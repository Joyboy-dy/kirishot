# Code of Conduct

Kirishot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on
what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and
publishing someone's private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, contact the maintainer, [Joyboy-dy](https://github.com/Joyboy-dy),
privately. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments,
commits or other contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
